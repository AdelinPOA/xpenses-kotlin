# DB Migrations (`db/migration`)

## Overview
Contains versioned Flyway SQL DDL migrations (`V1__init.sql` through `V110__fuel_prices_and_mileage_source.sql`) defining and evolving the PostgreSQL schema for users, user vehicles, categories, expense reports, expenses, receipts, advances, mileage records, insurance files, FX rates, fuel prices, refresh tokens, app settings, OCR jobs/comparisons/cache, IMAP ingestion ledger, storage migration tracking, and idempotency tables.

## Invariants
- `[V1__init.sql:categories.name]` & `[V1__init.sql:users.username]` — `categories.name` and `users.username` are unique and non-null (`users.email` has only a non-unique index in `[V17__add_email_index.sql:idx_users_email]`).
- `[V1__init.sql:fx_rates]` & `[V20__convert_monetary_to_numeric.sql:chk_fx_rate_positive]` & `[V30__persist_fx_source_date_and_reset_cache.sql:fx_rates]` — `fx_rates` enforces unique `(from_currency, to_currency, rate_date)`, positive `NUMERIC` `rate > 0`, and non-null `source_date` and `source`.
- `[V5__data_integrity_constraints.sql:chk_reports_status]` & `[V21__fix_expenses_status_check.sql:chk_expenses_status]` & `[V12__fix_advances_status_check.sql:chk_advances_status]` — Status values are constrained to `('draft', 'submitted', 'approved', 'rejected', 'paid')` on `expense_reports` and `expenses`, and `('paid', 'pending', 'rejected', 'requested')` on `advance_payments`.
- `[V5__data_integrity_constraints.sql:chk_users_role]` & `[V5__data_integrity_constraints.sql:chk_users_auth_source]` — `users.role` is restricted to `('technician', 'manager', 'chef_finance', 'mod')` and `users.auth_source` to `('local', 'ldap')`.
- `[V20__convert_monetary_to_numeric.sql:chk_expenses_amount_original]` & `[V31__add_mileage_fx_snapshot.sql:chk_mileage_amount_original_nonnegative]` & `[V5__data_integrity_constraints.sql:chk_reports_total_amount]` — Monetary amounts (`expenses`, `advance_payments`, `expense_reports.total_amount`, `mileage_records.amount_original`) and `mileage_records.distance_km` must be `>= 0` (with `NUMERIC` precision for money/rates), and `mileage_records.fx_rate > 0`.
- `[V103__durabilize_ocr_job_state.sql:ocr_jobs_stage_check]` & `[V103__durabilize_ocr_job_state.sql:ocr_jobs_status_check]` & `[V106__ocr_acceptance_and_recovery.sql:uq_ocr_jobs_user_acceptance_key]` — `ocr_jobs.stage` is restricted to `('OCR', 'EXPENSE', 'RECEIPT')`, `status` to `('PENDING', 'PROCESSING', 'RETRY_WAIT', 'PAUSED', 'PERMANENT_FAILURE', 'CANCELLED', 'COMPLETED', 'EXPIRED', 'UNRECOVERABLE')`, `attempt_count >= 0`, `report_link_status` to `('NOT_REQUESTED', 'PENDING', 'LINKED', 'CONFLICT')`, `acceptance_key` and `acceptance_fingerprint` must be simultaneously null or non-null, and `(user_id, acceptance_key)` is unique when non-null.
- `[V104__expense_save_idempotency.sql:expense_save_requests]` & `[V105__expense_verification.sql:expense_review_operations]` & `[V106__ocr_acceptance_and_recovery.sql:ocr_job_action_operations]` — Idempotency keys are unique per `(user_id, operation, request_key)`, `(user_id, request_key)`, and `(user_id, job_id, action, request_key)`; `expense_save_requests` intentionally has no FK to `expenses` so deleting an expense cannot reopen key replay.
- `[V105__expense_verification.sql:chk_expenses_verification_state]` — `expenses.verification_state` is restricted to `('NOT_REQUIRED', 'PENDING', 'VERIFIED')`, with `last_verified_by_user_id` set to `NULL` on verifier deletion.
- `[V108__user_vehicles_and_mileage_link.sql:uq_user_vehicles_default]` — At most one `user_vehicles` row per `user_id` may have `is_default = TRUE`; `mileage_records.vehicle_id` references `user_vehicles(id) ON DELETE SET NULL`.
- `[V110__fuel_prices_and_mileage_source.sql:uq_fuel_prices_type_date]` & `[V110__fuel_prices_and_mileage_source.sql:chk_fuel_prices_nonnegative]` — `fuel_prices` enforces unique `(fuel_type, price_date)` and `price_ron_per_liter >= 0`.

## Side Effects
- `[V1__init.sql:CREATE TABLE]` through `[V110__fuel_prices_and_mileage_source.sql:CREATE TABLE]` — Mutate PostgreSQL schema objects (tables, columns, `NUMERIC`/`TIMESTAMPTZ` type conversions, constraints, indexes, and drops of `alembic_version`, `mineru_*`/`easyocr_*` columns, and `inventory_items`/`inventory_requests`).
- `[V30__persist_fx_source_date_and_reset_cache.sql:DELETE FROM fx_rates]` — Deletes all existing cached rows in `fx_rates` during migration before enforcing `NOT NULL` on `source_date` and `source`.
- `[V31__add_mileage_fx_snapshot.sql:UPDATE mileage_records]` — Backfills existing `mileage_records` rows with `amount_original = amount_eur`, `currency_original = 'EUR'`, `fx_rate = 1`.
- `[V102__harden_imap_ingestion_recovery.sql:UPDATE imap_ingestion_messages]` — Backfills `acknowledgement_status = 'CONFIRMED'` and `acknowledged_at = COALESCE(processed_at, NOW())` for existing `PROCESSED` rows.
- `[V103__durabilize_ocr_job_state.sql:UPDATE ocr_jobs]` — Quarantines pre-existing `ocr_jobs` rows by copying `status` to `legacy_status`, setting `status = 'UNRECOVERABLE'` and `unrecoverable_reason = 'legacy_source_missing'`, resetting `stage = 'OCR'`, and clearing lease fields.
