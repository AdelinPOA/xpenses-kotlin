# Repositories

## Overview
Defines Spring Data JPA repository interfaces over PostgreSQL for all domain entities (`User`, `Category`, `ExpenseReport`, `Expense`, `Receipt`, `AdvancePayment`, `MileageRecord`, `InsuranceFile`, `FxRate`, `RefreshToken`, `AppSetting`, `OcrJob`, `OcrComparison`, `OcrResultCache`, `ExpenseReviewOperation`, and `OcrJobActionOperation`), including pessimistic/advisory locking, lease-fenced compare-and-set OCR job state transitions, and soft-delete/filter queries.

## Interface
- `[AdvancePaymentRepository.kt:AdvancePaymentRepository]` — `JpaRepository<AdvancePayment, Int>` with `findAllByReportId`, `findAllByUserId`, and `@EntityGraph` `findAllByStatus`.
- `[AppSettingRepository.kt:AppSettingRepository]` — `JpaRepository<AppSetting, String>` with native `acquireOcrConfigLock()`.
- `[CategoryRepository.kt:CategoryRepository]` — `JpaRepository<Category, Int>` with `findByName` and `findAllByIsActiveTrue`.
- `[ExpenseReportRepository.kt:ExpenseReportRepository]` — `JpaRepository<ExpenseReport, Int>` with `findByIdForUpdate`, `findAllByUserId`, `findByIdWithExpenses`, `findAllWithExpenses`, `findAllByUserIdWithExpenses`, `findAllByStatusWithExpenses`, `findReviewQueue`, `findAllByUserIdAndStatusWithExpenses`, `countByStatus`, `countByUserIdAndStatus`, `findFirstByUserIdAndKundenauftragAndStatusOrderByIdDesc`, `countByStatusGrouped`, `findLatestWithExpenses`, `findLatestByUserIdWithExpenses`, `findAllWithFiltersPaged`, `findDeletedAudit`, and `search`.
- `[ExpenseRepository.kt:ExpenseRepository]` — `JpaRepository<Expense, Int>` with `findAllByUserId`, `findAllByReportId`, `countByUserId`, `findAllLegacyReceiptPaths`, `findAllWithOcrTrainingData`, `findAllWithFilters`, `findAllWithFiltersPaged`, `findByIdForReview`, `findUnattachedByUserId`, `findAllByUserIdAndStatus`, `findAllByDateBetween`, `findAllByUserIdAndDateBetween`, `sumAmountEurByDateFrom`, `spendByCategorySince`, `findAllByUserIdAndDateTo`, `findRecentExpenses`, `findRecentExpensesByUserId`, `findDeletedAudit`, and `search`.
- `[ExpenseReviewOperationRepository.kt:ExpenseReviewOperationRepository]` — `JpaRepository<ExpenseReviewOperation, Long>` with `findByUser_IdAndRequestKey`.
- `[FuelPriceRepository.kt:FuelPriceRepository]` — `JpaRepository<FuelPrice, Int>` with `findByFuelTypeAndPriceDate` and `findTopByOrderByFetchedAtDesc`.
- `[FxRateRepository.kt:FxRateRepository]` — `JpaRepository<FxRate, Int>` with `findByFromCurrencyAndToCurrencyAndRateDate` and `findTopByOrderByFetchedAtDesc`.
- `[InsuranceFileRepository.kt:InsuranceFileRepository]` — `JpaRepository<InsuranceFile, Int>` with `findAllFilePaths`, `findAllByUserIdOrderByCreatedAtDesc`, and `findAllByOrderByCreatedAtDesc`.
- `[MileageRecordRepository.kt:MileageRecordRepository]` — `JpaRepository<MileageRecord, Int>` with `findAllByReportId` and `findAllByUserId`.
- `[OcrComparisonRepository.kt:OcrComparisonRepository]` — `JpaRepository<OcrComparison, Int>` with `findAllOrdered`, `findBestCachedComparison`, and `updateGeminiResult`.
- `[OcrJobActionOperationRepository.kt:OcrJobActionOperationRepository]` — `JpaRepository<OcrJobActionOperation, Long>` with `findByUser_IdAndJob_IdAndActionAndRequestKey`.
- `[OcrJobRepository.kt:OcrJobRepository]` — `JpaRepository<OcrJob, Int>` with `findByUser_IdAndAcceptanceKey`, `findFirstByUser_IdAndExpenseIdOrderByCreatedAtDesc`, `findAllByUser_IdOrderByCreatedAtDesc`, `findByIdForUpdate`, `findSourceCleanupCandidates`, `lockForSourceCleanup`, `isSourcePathShared`, `findAllSourcePaths`, `findClaimableJobIds`, `findExpiredJobIds`, `completeOcrStage`, `completeExpenseStage`, `completeReceiptStage`, `cancelClaimed`, `claimEligible`, `completeClaimedStage`, `recordPermanentFailure`, `reclaimExpired`, and `recordTransientFailure`.
- `[OcrResultCacheRepository.kt:OcrResultCacheRepository]` — `JpaRepository<OcrResultCache, Int>` with `findBySha256AndProviderAndModelAndContractVersion`, `acquireScanLock`, and `deleteExpiredBatch`.
- `[ReceiptRepository.kt:ReceiptRepository]` — `JpaRepository<Receipt, Int>` with `findAllByExpenseId` and `findAllFilePaths`.
- `[RefreshTokenRepository.kt:RefreshTokenRepository]` — `JpaRepository<RefreshToken, Int>` with `findByTokenHash`, `findByTokenHashForUpdate`, `deleteByUserId`, and `deleteExpiredAndRotated`.
- `[UserRepository.kt:UserRepository]` — `JpaRepository<User, Int>` with `findAllSignaturePaths`, `findByUsername`, `findByEmail`, `findByIdentifier`, `findAllByAuthSourceAndIsActive`, `findAllByRoleInAndIsActive`, and `findAllByOrderByCreatedAt`.

## Invariants
- `[AppSettingRepository.kt:acquireOcrConfigLock]` & `[OcrResultCacheRepository.kt:acquireScanLock]` — Use PostgreSQL transaction-scoped advisory locks (`pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended(..., 0))`) to serialize OCR config writes and concurrent scans per lock key; must execute inside short transactions without holding locks across external network calls.
- `[ExpenseReportRepository.kt:findByIdForUpdate]` & `[ExpenseRepository.kt:findByIdForReview]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:findByIdForUpdate]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:lockForSourceCleanup]` & `[RefreshTokenRepository.kt:findByTokenHashForUpdate]` — Enforce `@Lock(LockModeType.PESSIMISTIC_WRITE)` row serialization on report OCR promotion, expense review, OCR job state/cleanup mutations, and refresh token rotation.
- `[OcrJobRepository.kt:claimEligible]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:completeOcrStage]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:completeExpenseStage]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:completeReceiptStage]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:completeClaimedStage]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:recordPermanentFailure]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:recordTransientFailure]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:reclaimExpired]` — Compare-and-set lease fencing requires matching `stage`, `status = 'PROCESSING'`, `lease_token = :leaseToken`, and `lease_until > :now` (or `lease_until <= :now` for `reclaimExpired`), returning affected row count `1` on ownership success.
- `[OcrJobRepository.kt:findSourceCleanupCandidates]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:cancelClaimed]` — Source cleanup only targets terminal statuses (`COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`, `PERMANENT_FAILURE`, `UNRECOVERABLE`, `EXPIRED`) past `expires_at` with no active lease; cancellation requires owner `user_id` and `status IN ('PENDING', 'RETRY_WAIT', 'PROCESSING')`.
- `[ExpenseReportRepository.kt:findReviewQueue]` — Filters strictly to `r.status = 'submitted'` and excludes self-review (`u.id <> :actorId`).
- `[ExpenseReportRepository.kt:findDeletedAudit]` & `[ExpenseRepository.kt:findDeletedAudit]` — Native SQL `WHERE deleted = true` queries bypass Hibernate soft-delete filtering for audit inspection; `[ExpenseRepository.kt:findAllLegacyReceiptPaths]` filters `e.deleted = false AND e.receiptPath IS NOT NULL`.
- `[RefreshTokenRepository.kt:deleteExpiredAndRotated]` — Preserves rotated tokens inside the rotation grace window (`r.rotatedAt >= :graceCutoff`), deleting only expired (`r.expiresAt < :now`) or past-grace rotated tokens.
- `[ExpenseReviewOperationRepository.kt:findByUser_IdAndRequestKey]` & `[OcrJobActionOperationRepository.kt:findByUser_IdAndJob_IdAndActionAndRequestKey]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:findByUser_IdAndAcceptanceKey]` & `[OcrResultCacheRepository.kt:findBySha256AndProviderAndModelAndContractVersion]` & `[FxRateRepository.kt:findByFromCurrencyAndToCurrencyAndRateDate]` & `[UserRepository.kt:findByIdentifier]` — Rely on database uniqueness for idempotency/cache keys; `findByIdentifier` prioritizes active users and lowest `id` first.

## Side Effects
- `[AppSettingRepository.kt:acquireOcrConfigLock]` & `[OcrResultCacheRepository.kt:acquireScanLock]` — Acquire PostgreSQL transaction-scoped advisory locks via native SQL.
- `[ExpenseReportRepository.kt:findByIdForUpdate]` & `[ExpenseRepository.kt:findByIdForReview]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:findByIdForUpdate]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:lockForSourceCleanup]` & `[RefreshTokenRepository.kt:findByTokenHashForUpdate]` — Acquire `PESSIMISTIC_WRITE` row locks in PostgreSQL.
- `[OcrJobRepository.kt:claimEligible]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:completeOcrStage]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:completeExpenseStage]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:completeReceiptStage]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:completeClaimedStage]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:cancelClaimed]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:recordPermanentFailure]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:reclaimExpired]` & `[OcrJobRepository.kt:recordTransientFailure]` — Execute `@Modifying(clearAutomatically = true, flushAutomatically = true) @Transactional` native `UPDATE` statements on `ocr_jobs`.
- `[OcrComparisonRepository.kt:updateGeminiResult]` & `[OcrResultCacheRepository.kt:deleteExpiredBatch]` — Execute `@Modifying @Transactional` updates on `OcrComparison` and bounded native `DELETE` batches on `ocr_result_cache`.
- `[RefreshTokenRepository.kt:deleteByUserId]` & `[RefreshTokenRepository.kt:deleteExpiredAndRotated]` — Delete `RefreshToken` rows in PostgreSQL.
