# Controllers

## Overview
Exposes the REST API endpoints under `/api/**` for authentication, expense reports, expenses, bulk uploads, advances, mileage, OCR receipt scanning and job recovery, search, review queue, dashboard aggregations, FX quotes, categories, insurance files, user signatures, user administration, system/integration settings, and health checks.

## Interface
- `[AdvanceController.kt:AdvanceController]` — Routes `GET /api/reports/{reportId}/advances`, `POST /api/reports/{reportId}/advances`, `PATCH /api/reports/{reportId}/advances/{advanceId}`, `DELETE /api/reports/{reportId}/advances/{advanceId}`, `POST /api/reports/{reportId}/advances/{advanceId}/pay`, `POST /api/reports/{reportId}/advances/{advanceId}/reject`, and `GET /api/advances/pending`.
- `[AppSettingsController.kt:AppSettingsController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/settings")` routes for `/ui` (`GET`/`PATCH`), `/daily-allowance-rate` (`GET`/`PATCH`), `/mileage-rates` (`GET`/`PATCH`), `/fx-margins` (`GET`/`PATCH`), `/fx-providers` (`GET`/`PATCH`), `/fx-providers/check` (`POST`), `/fx-providers/test` (`POST`), `/fuel-providers` (`GET`/`PATCH`), `/fuel-prices/check` (`POST`), `/ocr` (`GET`/`PATCH`), `/ocr/models/test` (`POST`), `/email` (`GET`/`PATCH`), `/email/test` (`POST`), `/imap` (`GET`/`PATCH`), `/imap/test` (`POST`), `/imap/simulate` (`POST`), `/imap/ingestion` (`GET`), `/imap/ingestion/retry` (`POST`), `/storage` (`GET`/`PATCH`), `/storage/test` (`POST`), `/storage/migration-status` (`GET`), `/receipts/scan` (`POST`), and `/receipts/clean` (`POST`).
- `[AuthController.kt:AuthController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/auth")` routes `POST /login`, `POST /refresh`, `POST /logout`, `POST /change-password`, and `GET /me`.
- `[BulkExpenseController.kt:BulkExpenseController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/expenses")` route `POST /bulk-upload` accepting `files: List<MultipartFile>` and `Idempotency-Key` header.
- `[CategoryController.kt:CategoryController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/categories")` routes `GET` (`active_only`), `POST`, `PATCH /{categoryId}`, and `DELETE /{categoryId}`; DTOs `CategoryCreateRequest` and `CategoryUpdateRequest`.
- `[DashboardController.kt:DashboardController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/dashboard")` routes `GET` (`all`) and `GET /manager`.
- `[DelegationParsingController.kt:DelegationParsingController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/settings/imap")` routes `GET /parsing`, `PATCH /parsing`, and `POST /preview`; DTOs `DelegationPreviewIn`, `DelegationPreviewOut`.
- `[ExpenseController.kt:ExpenseController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/expenses")` routes `GET` (filtered/paged), `POST`, `GET /{expenseId}`, `PATCH /{expenseId}`, `PUT /{expenseId}/review`, `POST /{expenseId}/duplicate`, `DELETE /{expenseId}`, `POST /{expenseId}/receipts`, `POST /{expenseId}/receipt`, `DELETE /{expenseId}/receipts/{receiptId}`, `GET /{expenseId}/receipts/{receiptId}`, `GET /{expenseId}/receipt`, and `GET /export`.
- `[FuelPriceController.kt:FuelPriceController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/fuel-prices")` route `GET /status`.
- `[FxController.kt:FxController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/fx")` routes `GET /quote` (`from`, `date`) and `GET /status`.
- `[HealthController.kt:HealthController]` — `GET /api/health` returning `{"status": "ok"}`.
- `[InsuranceController.kt:InsuranceController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/insurance")` routes `GET`, `GET /{fileId}`, `POST`, `GET /{fileId}/download`, and `DELETE /{fileId}`; DTO `InsuranceFileOut`.
- `[LdapSettingsController.kt:LdapSettingsController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/settings/ldap")` routes `GET`, `PATCH`, `POST /test`, and `POST /sync`; DTOs `LdapConfigIn`, `LdapConfigOut`.
- `[MileageController.kt:MileageController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/reports/{reportId}/mileage")` routes `GET`, `GET /trip-sheet`, `POST`, `PATCH /{mileageId}`, and `DELETE /{mileageId}`.
- `[OcrController.kt:OcrController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/ocr")` routes `POST /scan`, `GET /status/{jobId}`, `GET /jobs`, `GET /jobs/{jobId}`, `POST /jobs/{jobId}/retry`, `POST /jobs/{jobId}/complete-manually`, `DELETE /jobs/{jobId}`, and `GET /comparison` (`OcrComparisonRow`).
- `[ReportController.kt:ReportController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/reports")` routes `GET`, `POST`, `POST /import-order/preview`, `POST /import-order`, `GET /{reportId}`, `PATCH /{reportId}`, `DELETE /{reportId}`, `POST /{reportId}/attach`, `POST /{reportId}/submit`, `POST /{reportId}/recall`, `POST /{reportId}/pay`, `POST /{reportId}/approve`, `POST /{reportId}/reject`, `POST /bulk-status`, `POST /{reportId}/sign`, `GET /{reportId}/export`, `GET /{reportId}/receipts-zip`, `GET /export`, and `GET /{reportId}/export-csv`.
- `[ReportOwnership.kt:ReportOwnership]` — `@Component` exposing `verifyOwnership(reportId: Int, currentUser: User): ExpenseReport`.
- `[ReviewQueueController.kt:ReviewQueueController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/review-queue")` route `GET` (`@Transactional(readOnly = true)`).
- `[SearchController.kt:SearchController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/search")` route `GET` (`q`, `all`, `@Transactional(readOnly = true)`).
- `[SignatureController.kt:SignatureController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/users/me/signature")` routes `GET`, `PUT`, and `DELETE`; DTO `SignaturePayload`.
- `[UserController.kt:UserController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/users")` routes `POST`, `GET`, `GET /{userId}`, `PUT /{userId}/role`, `PUT /{userId}/active`, and `PATCH /me`.
- `[UserVehicleController.kt:UserVehicleController]` — `@RequestMapping("/api/users/me/vehicles")` routes `GET`, `POST`, `PATCH /{vehicleId}`, and `DELETE /{vehicleId}`.

## Invariants
- `[ReportOwnership.kt:verifyOwnership]` & `[ExpenseController.kt:ExpenseController]` & `[ReviewQueueController.kt:getReviewQueue]` & `[SearchController.kt:search]` & `[SignatureController.kt:SignatureController]` — Endpoints reading `currentUser` request attribute require authentication (`401` when missing) and enforce `Authorization` role/ownership checks (`403` on unauthorized access, `404` when target entity is missing).
- `[AppSettingsController.kt:AppSettingsController]` & `[DelegationParsingController.kt:DelegationParsingController]` & `[LdapSettingsController.kt:LdapSettingsController]` & `[OcrController.kt:getOcrComparisons]` & `[UserController.kt:createUser]` — Administrative endpoints enforce `@PreAuthorize("hasRole('mod')")`, `@PreAuthorize("hasRole('chef_finance')")`, or `@PreAuthorize("hasAnyRole('mod', 'chef_finance')")`, masking/retaining stored secrets (`[AppSettingsController.kt:readStorageSettings]`, `[LdapSettingsController.kt:mapToOut]`).
- `[AuthController.kt:login]` & `[AuthController.kt:refresh]` & `[AuthController.kt:changePassword]` — `login` runs constant-time dummy BCrypt checks on unknown usernames and rejects inactive users (`403`); `refresh` locks the token row with `findByTokenHashForUpdate` and revokes all user sessions on replay beyond the 30s `REFRESH_ROTATION_GRACE_SECONDS` grace window; `changePassword` requires `authSource == "local"` and `newPassword.length >= 8`.
- `[AdvanceController.kt:createAdvance]` & `[MileageController.kt:MileageController]` & `[UserVehicleController.kt:UserVehicleController]` & `[ReportController.kt:ReportController]` — Financial mutations validate report state (`draft` / non-`paid`/non-`rejected`), advance type/status (`requested`/`paid`), delegate mileage (`endTime > startTime` `422`), vehicle, and odometer validation/advancement to `VehicleMileage`, and delegate report CRUD/lifecycle/exports/order-import to `ExpenseReports`.
- `[BulkExpenseController.kt:bulkUploadExpenses]` & `[OcrController.kt:scanReceipt]` — Validate upload extensions (`{jpg, jpeg, png, pdf, webp}`), 10MB max size, `OcrService.isValidOcrContent` magic bytes, per-user OCR rate limits (`rl:ocr_quota:user:{userId}`, 30 requests / 60s -> `429`), and `Idempotency-Key` conflicts (`409`).
- `[ExpenseController.kt:reviewExpense]` & `[OcrController.kt:retryJob]` & `[OcrController.kt:completeJobManually]` & `[ReportController.kt:createReport]` — Enforce valid `Idempotency-Key` headers (mandatory on `reviewExpense`, `retryJob`, `completeJobManually`) and map version conflicts to `409 Conflict`.
- `[ExpenseController.kt:getSingleReceipt]` & `[InsuranceController.kt:downloadFile]` — Set `SafeMediaType.safeDisposition` and `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` on file downloads; `[SignatureController.kt:putSignature]` validates `data:image/png;base64,`, Base64 decoding, `<= 512000` bytes, and 8-byte PNG magic header.
- `[CategoryController.kt:createCategory]` & `[UserController.kt:createUser]` — Enforce unique category names (`409`) and unique usernames (`409`), valid roles (`{technician, manager, chef_finance, mod}`), and languages (`{en, ro}`).

## Side Effects
- `[AdvanceController.kt:AdvanceController]` & `[MileageController.kt:MileageController]` & `[UserVehicleController.kt:UserVehicleController]` & `[ExpenseController.kt:ExpenseController]` & `[ReportController.kt:ReportController]` & `[CategoryController.kt:CategoryController]` — Persist and delete financial entities, advances, and categories in PostgreSQL (invoking `fxService.getRate` and dispatching notifications via `notificationAdapter.dispatch`), while `MileageController` and `UserVehicleController` delegate to `VehicleMileage`, `ReportController` delegates to `ExpenseReports`, and `SearchController` projects reports via `ReportProjector`.
- `[AuthController.kt:login]` & `[AuthController.kt:refresh]` & `[AuthController.kt:logout]` & `[UserController.kt:updateActive]` — Mutate `RefreshToken` rows and `User.tokenVersion`, authenticate via external LDAP when configured, and issue/clear `HttpOnly` `access_token` and `refresh_token` cookies.
- `[BulkExpenseController.kt:bulkUploadExpenses]` & `[OcrController.kt:persistSourceAndDispatch]` & `[InsuranceController.kt:uploadFile]` & `[SignatureController.kt:putSignature]` — Store/delete binary files via `StorageService` or on disk (`signaturesDir`), record rate-limit counters in `RateLimitStore`, and trigger synchronous or asynchronous OCR (`ocrJobPipeline.processOcr`).
- `[AppSettingsController.kt:AppSettingsController]` & `[LdapSettingsController.kt:LdapSettingsController]` & `[DelegationParsingController.kt:saveSettings]` — Persist encrypted/system settings, trigger receipt storage migration or orphan cleanup, and execute external SMTP, IMAP, LDAP, FX provider, storage, and AI model connectivity checks/syncs.
- `[ReportController.kt:exportReportPdf]` & `[ReportController.kt:downloadReceiptsZip]` & `[ReportController.kt:exportReports]` & `[ExpenseController.kt:exportExpenses]` — Generate PDF, ZIP, CSV, and XLSX exports (via `ExpenseReports` for reports and `ExpenseService` for expenses).
